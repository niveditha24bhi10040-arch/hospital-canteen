Quick Prep Guide

  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
  • Find a quiet, well-lit room for your call.
  • Have your latest lab reports (from Lab Portal) ready.
  • Keep a list of current medications nearby.

Emergency? If you are experiencing a life-threatening situation, please call emergency services immediately rather than booking a consultation.
